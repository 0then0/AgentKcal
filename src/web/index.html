From usage to energy

Each token bucket has its own Wh per million tokens. We multiply the sum by PUE, the data center overhead factor. Model patterns select a configurable profile; otherwise the explicit fallback applies.

Wh = PUE × Σ(tokens × Wh_per_million / 1,000,000)

With GPU telemetry, GPU seconds × supplied watts / 3,600 replaces the token calculation. Wall-clock request latency is never treated as GPU time.

Know what the numbers mean

Default coefficients are illustrative assumptions, not measured model benchmarks. The low/high range is a sensitivity scenario, not a statistical confidence interval. Real energy depends on hardware, batching, model, context and infrastructure.

Training, embodied energy, network traffic and your device are excluded. Kilocalories are a physical energy conversion: kcal = Wh × 3,600 / 4,184.

Food equivalents divide the same estimated kcal by the energy in a stated portion. These are visual comparisons, not food consumed.

Edit energy in your local config.json to calibrate profiles. Estimates are recalculated from the original token counts.
